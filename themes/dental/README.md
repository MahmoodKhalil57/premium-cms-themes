# PremiumCMS Dental theme

A complete private-practice website for PremiumCMS: treatments with fees and online deposits, one-minute booking with clinician availability, signed new-patient intake forms, a before/after smile gallery, live Google reviews, FAQ structured data, a cookie-consent banner and a small dental shop.

Served by PremiumCMS as is, or forked to your GitHub and hosted on GitHub Pages with one-click theme updates. Everything on the pages is designed in the page builder; the seed in `seed/` also sets up the booking plugin (treatments, team, reminders and recalls), the intake and contact forms and the Site Kit business profile.

## Plugins

- `premium-bookings` — treatments, clinicians (CMS users), online booking, reminders and recalls
- `premium-commerce` — deposits and the shop (bookings are paid through its checkout)
- `premium-forms` — contact + signed intake forms
- `premium-site-kit` — schema.org Dentist profile, analytics, consent, Google reviews

## Customise

Admin → Pages (copy & layout), Plugins → Bookings → Services and Staff & resources (fees, deposits, hours), Plugins → Site Kit → Settings (address, opening hours, GA4, Google Place ID).
